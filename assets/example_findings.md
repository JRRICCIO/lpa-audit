# Example findings (two worked examples)

These are the level of specificity expected for every finding. Read them before producing output to calibrate your tone, length, and depth.

## Example RED finding

### A. AIFMD II regulatory compliance

#### 3. Liquidity management tools

- **Clause:** Section 14.2 (Redemption mechanics), page 38.
- **Quote:** "[document is silent on liquidity management tools beyond suspension of redemptions]"
- **Standard:** AIFMD II Article 16(2b); ESMA Guidelines ESMA34-671404336-1364 (March 2026); Annex V of AIFMD II.
- **Severity:** RED.
- **Issue:** The LPA describes an open-ended structure with quarterly redemptions on 60 days notice, but selects only one liquidity management tool (suspension of redemptions). AIFMD II requires open-ended AIFs to select at least two LMTs from the harmonised EU list and incorporate them into the constitutional documents. As drafted, the AIFM cannot deploy gates, swing pricing, or anti-dilution levies even in stressed conditions. This is a regulatory non-compliance that blocks the AIFM's CSSF approval at the latest version of the file.
- **Suggested redline:** Insert a new clause 14.3: "In addition to the suspension of redemptions described in Clause 14.2, the AIFM shall be authorised to apply any one or more of the following liquidity management tools, in accordance with the procedure set out in Schedule 4: (a) redemption gates limiting the aggregate redemption amount in any redemption period to 10% of NAV; (b) swing pricing applied symmetrically to subscriptions and redemptions; (c) anti-dilution levies. Schedule 4 shall set out the activation triggers, the AIFM's authority to apply each tool, and the notification procedure to Limited Partners."
- **Owner of fix:** Counsel to GP (LPA drafter), with AIFM input on activation triggers.

## Example AMBER finding

### B. ILPA Principles 3.0 market alignment

#### 11. Key person provisions

- **Clause:** Section 9.3, page 31.
- **Quote:** "If two or more of the Key Persons cease to be employed by the General Partner or its affiliates due to death, disability, or termination of employment, the Investment Period shall be suspended."
- **Standard:** ILPA Principles 3.0 § 4.2 (key person provisions should include a dedication-of-time test, not only employment status).
- **Severity:** AMBER.
- **Issue:** The trigger captures departures and incapacities but does not capture the case where a key person is technically still employed by the GP but is no longer dedicating substantially all business time to the Fund (e.g., they have taken on a competing mandate or stepped into a non-investment role at the GP). This is the most common real-world key person failure mode and the ILPA Model LPA addresses it explicitly. The suspension on this LPA can be circumvented by repurposing a key person within the GP.
- **Suggested redline:** Replace the trigger with: "If at any time two or more of the Key Persons (i) cease to be employed by the General Partner or its affiliates, (ii) become subject to a disability that prevents them from substantially performing their duties, or (iii) cease to dedicate substantially all of their business time to the affairs of the Fund and its successor funds, in each case as determined by a majority vote of the Limited Partner Advisory Committee, the Investment Period shall automatically be suspended."
- **Owner of fix:** LP-side negotiation. Likely accepted by GP for first-time funds; may be resisted by established managers.

## Calibration takeaways

- The quote is a direct, short excerpt. When the LPA is silent on a mandatory item, write "[document is silent on X]" and keep the finding.
- The standard cites the exact authority (statute, regulation, ILPA section). Do not write "industry standard" without a citation.
- The issue is two to four sentences of plain English explaining the consequence, not just restating the clause.
- The suggested redline is a paste-ready paragraph, not an instruction to "improve the language."
- The owner of fix is concrete (counsel to GP, AIFM, LP-side negotiation, LPAC).
