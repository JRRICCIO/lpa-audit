# AIFMD II checklist (areas 1 to 7)

Authority: Directive (EU) 2024/927, in force 16 April 2026. Phased implementation of certain Regulatory Technical Standards (RTS) deferred to 16 April 2027 (notably the detailed RTS on liquidity management tools and enhanced Annex IV reporting granularity).

Luxembourg transposition: Bill of Law 8628, published Memorial A on 9 March 2026, effective 16 April 2026. No gold-plating.

Companion regulation: CSSF Circular 25/901 (19 December 2025) consolidating the SIF, SICAR, and Part II UCI framework.

## 1. Leverage limits and calculation

Authority: Article 15(4b) AIFMD as amended; ESMA RTS on leverage calculation.

For loan-originating AIFs:
- 175% of NAV for open-ended structures
- 300% of NAV for closed-ended structures
- Calculated on the commitment method (Article 8 of Delegated Regulation 231/2013)

Check:
- Does the LPA define leverage consistently with the commitment method?
- Is the cap stated in a way that survives currency moves and capital calls?
- Are derivatives and securities-financing transactions captured?
- For non-loan-originating AIFs, does the LPA state the manager-determined leverage cap and the reporting trigger?

Red flags:
- "Leverage will not exceed X%" without defining the calculation method.
- A cap that applies "where reasonably practicable" with no objective trigger.
- No mention of leverage at all in an LO-AIF (RED, silence).

## 2. Loan origination framework

Authority: Article 15a, 16a AIFMD as amended; ESMA RTS on open-ended LO-AIFs.

Mandatory elements:
- Single-borrower concentration: 20% of capital, with carveouts for sovereigns and credit institutions.
- 5% retention requirement: AIFM must retain at least 5% of notional value of any loan originated and subsequently transferred.
- Prohibition on the "originate-to-distribute" model (AIFM cannot manage an AIF that originates loans solely for the purpose of selling them).
- Credit risk policies and processes, reviewed at least annually.
- Prohibition on lending to specific counterparties (the AIFM itself, its delegates, affiliated entities, depositary, except with strict carveouts).

Check:
- Does the LPA include the 5% retention as an obligation of the GP / AIFM?
- Single-borrower limit specified, with the formula and the exceptions?
- Annual review cadence for credit risk policy named?
- Prohibitions on intra-group lending stated?

Red flags:
- Loan origination authority granted with no retention requirement (RED).
- "Concentration limits as determined by the GP from time to time" (AMBER, vague).
- Silence on annual credit risk policy review for an LO-AIF (RED, silence).

## 3. Liquidity management tools (LMTs)

Authority: Article 16(2b) AIFMD as amended; ESMA Guidelines on liquidity management tools of UCITS and open-ended AIFs (ESMA34-671404336-1364, March 2026).

For open-ended AIFs:
- Mandatory selection of at least two LMTs from the harmonised EU list (Annex V).
- The list: redemption gates, swing pricing, anti-dilution levies, notice periods, redemption fees, redemption in kind, side pockets, suspension of redemptions, partial redemption suspensions.
- Activation and deactivation procedures must be documented.
- Suspension of redemptions and side pockets must be available regardless of the two chosen tools.
- LMTs must be incorporated into the fund's constitutional documents (so the LPA itself, or a clearly cross-referenced schedule).

Check:
- For an open-ended structure, are at least two LMTs named in the LPA?
- Are the activation triggers, the AIFM's authority to activate, and the LP notification procedure all specified?
- Are LMTs consistent with the redemption terms (a daily-dealing fund with no swing pricing or gates is a red flag)?

Red flags:
- Open-ended structure with no LMTs in the LPA (RED, regulatory non-compliance).
- LMTs named but the LPA fails to give the AIFM the authority to activate them (RED).
- Suspension of redemptions phrased as discretionary with no triggers (AMBER).

## 4. Delegation arrangements

Authority: Article 20 AIFMD as amended; ESMA Guidelines on delegation.

Mandatory elements:
- AIFM must retain substantive presence: at least the minimum FTEs necessary to exercise judgement on delegated functions.
- AIFM cannot become a "letter-box entity" (a shell that has formally delegated all material functions).
- Delegation must be in writing, with clear allocation of responsibility, oversight cadence, and rights of recall.
- AIFM must track FTEs allocated to portfolio/risk management vs FTEs allocated to delegation monitoring.
- Enhanced Annex IV reporting on delegation arrangements, staffing, due diligence outcomes (full compliance with RTS deferred to 16 April 2027).

Check:
- Does the LPA name the AIFM and confirm it is appointed (not merely "to be appointed")?
- Are the AIFM's delegated functions enumerated (portfolio management, risk management, administration, marketing, distribution, loan origination)?
- Is the AIFM's oversight obligation expressed in a way that survives the "letter-box" test?

Red flags:
- "The AIFM may delegate any or all of its functions to such persons as it deems appropriate" without further constraint (AMBER).
- An AIFM that delegates portfolio management AND risk management AND administration with no retained functions (RED).
- Silence on whether the AIFM has been appointed (RED, silence).

## 5. Conflict of interest

Authority: Article 14 AIFMD as amended; ESMA Guidelines on conflicts of interest.

Mandatory elements:
- Identification of structural and transactional conflicts.
- Mitigation measures (Chinese walls, segregation of functions, employee training).
- Disclosure to LPs of material residual conflicts.
- Procedure for conflicted decisions (typically LPAC consent for affiliate transactions, related-party transactions, cross-fund deals).
- Annual review of the conflicts of interest policy.

Check:
- Does the LPA describe the conflicts policy or cross-reference it?
- Are affiliate transactions subject to LPAC consent?
- Are cross-fund transactions (warehousing, co-investment allocation, follow-on rounds in the same portfolio company) addressed?

Red flags:
- "The GP may resolve conflicts in its sole discretion" (RED, ILPA-non-conforming).
- No LPAC consent required for affiliate transactions (RED under ILPA 3.0).
- Silence on cross-fund transactions (AMBER).

## 6. Reporting

Authority: Article 24 AIFMD as amended; Annex IV reporting template.

Enhanced for AIFMD II:
- Granular data on delegation arrangements (FTEs, names of delegates, scope).
- Staffing data on the AIFM (organisational chart, dedicated headcount).
- Due diligence outcomes on delegates.
- Sustainability data where applicable (Article 8 / Article 9 SFDR funds).

Check:
- Does the LPA bind the GP to provide the data the AIFM needs for Annex IV reporting?
- Are LPs informed of reporting frequency to the CSSF?
- Are LP-facing reporting obligations (quarterly NAV, capital account statements, annual audited financials) clearly stated?

Red flags:
- LP reporting frequency vague ("from time to time") (AMBER).
- No commitment from the GP to provide AIFM-required data (AMBER, this is usually in the AIFM agreement but worth flagging if absent).

## 7. Sustainability disclosures (when applicable)

Authority: Regulation (EU) 2019/2088 (SFDR); Delegated Regulation 2022/1288 (SFDR RTS).

For Article 8 funds (promote E/S characteristics):
- Pre-contractual disclosure aligned with SFDR Annex II template.
- Periodic reporting on E/S characteristics achieved.

For Article 9 funds (sustainable investment objective):
- Pre-contractual disclosure aligned with SFDR Annex III template.
- Demonstration of how the sustainable investment objective is achieved.
- No significant harm (DNSH) assessment.

Check:
- If the LPA references an SFDR classification, is the classification supported by the operational sections of the LPA (investment policy, ESG diligence, divestment triggers)?
- Are sustainability data obligations from portfolio companies described?

Red flags:
- LPA claims Article 9 but investment policy is general PE / VC with no sustainable objective (RED, mis-classification).
- LPA claims Article 8 but no ESG diligence or reporting machinery (AMBER to RED depending on materiality).
